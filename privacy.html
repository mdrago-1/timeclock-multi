Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Time Rogue (“we”, “our”, “us”) provides a staff time-clock, scheduling and employee records service for UK businesses. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

  • Account data — email address and password when you sign up.
  • Employee records — names, contact details, start dates, pay rates, and other information you enter for your staff.
  • Time and attendance data — clock-in/out times, schedules, holiday records, and related notes.
  • Documents — files you upload (for example starter forms, ID documents, or handbooks).
  • Payment data — processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Technical data — basic logs such as browser type and IP address for security and troubleshooting.

How we use data

  • To provide the time-clock, scheduling, and employee management features you use.
  • To create and manage your account and employee records.
  • To process payments via Stripe.
  • To send essential service emails (for example password resets or account notices).
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the service.

Sharing data

We do not sell your data. We share data only with:

  • Supabase — for database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Stripe — for payment processing.
  • Resend — for sending transactional emails.
  • Service providers who help us host and operate the service, under confidentiality obligations.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. Some records may be retained where required by law.

Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email us at support@timerogue.app.
