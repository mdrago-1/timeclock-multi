Delete your Time Rogue account

This page is for the Time Rogue app at timerogue.app. You can ask us to delete your business account, or to delete some data without closing the account.

How to request deletion

  1. Email hello@timerogue.app from the email address on the Time Rogue account.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Time Rogue account to close the whole account, or Delete some Time Rogue data if you only want certain records removed.
  3. Include the business name and say what you want deleted. To delete only some data, name the employee or the records.

We will confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What we delete

  • The business login and password.
  • Employee profiles, starter forms, documents, clock records, schedules and holiday records.
  • Shop settings, including the keypad login and logo.

What we may keep

We may keep invoices and payment records for up to 6 years where UK tax law requires it. Those records are not used for anything else. Backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle, within 90 days.
